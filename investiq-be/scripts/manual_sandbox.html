1. Prepare accounts and contests

  1. Register a test account in the sandbox frontend. Open its verification email in Mailpit.
  2. To test the admin flow, enter that verified sandbox email below, enable admin, then reload the frontend.
  3. Open Contests → Administration → Add InvestIQ contest. Keep the suggested October 15–16, 2026 schedule, save the draft, then publish it.
  4. Use a separate browser profile/incognito window for another learner at http://127.0.0.1:5175. Register, verify, join the public contest and confirm a portfolio. A saved draft alone does not enter rankings.
  5. Submit different portfolios from two learners to compare standings. Finish all entry work before advancing.

2. Advance the simulation

Each step is immediate. After advancing, use Refresh results in Performance/Leaderboard, or reload the frontend. At the first close, results are still pending publication; at the valuation step, actual fixture prices produce standings. Final valuation completes the contest.

The clock moves forward only. To repeat entry/submission tests, stop this runner with Ctrl+C and restart it for a fresh database.
